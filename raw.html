The raw escape hatch

SDL covers the whole current SurrealQL definition grammar — but not every byte the engine accepts, and tomorrow's engine may accept more. raw is the escape hatch: a block whose contents pass through to the DDL byte for byte.

The mechanism

Everything between the braces is captured verbatim — comments, spacing, spelling — and emitted unchanged (only the leading and trailing edges are trimmed):

Use it for legal SurrealQL shapes outside SDL's surface — legacy index options, a statement SDL has not grown yet — so your schema file stays complete while the language catches up.

The rules raw plays by

  • Top level only. raw is a reserved word inside every block — no nesting. (A field literally named raw escapes via backticks or the field prefix.)
  • No doc comments. /// above a raw block is rejected — there is no COMMENT clause to hang one on.
  • Validation is advisory. A body the SurrealQL parser rejects is still carried and still emitted — the lint below tells you, and never blocks. Rule 8 exists for exactly the class of bodies whose validity the pinned parser cannot confirm.
  • Your responsibility at apply time. The compiler passes the body through; the engine is the final judge.

When raw body is broken

surform lint names the body's problem; check and build still succeed.
